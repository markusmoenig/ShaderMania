# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 30, 2026_

ShaderMania is built by Markus Moenig. This policy explains what is collected, how data is used, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Project data**: Shader projects, images, and exports stay on your device or in your own iCloud storage. They are not collected by Markus Moenig.
- **Log and crash data**: If an app error occurs, platform diagnostics or third-party services may provide log data such as device type, operating system version, app configuration, time and date, and related statistics.
- **Support messages**: When you contact Markus Moenig, the information you provide, such as your email address and message content, is used to respond.

## How we use data

- Provide and improve ShaderMania.
- Maintain app performance and stability.
- Respond to support requests.
- Monitor basic, aggregated metrics provided by the App Store.

## Storage and retention

- Your creative files remain on your device or your iCloud account.
- Crash diagnostics, if provided, are retained by the platform provider or diagnostic service and may be made available in aggregate.
- Support correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle your request.

## Sharing

Personal information is not sold or rented. Limited data may be shared with service providers, such as Apple, for app distribution, payments, diagnostics, analytics, and App Store services.

## Children

ShaderMania is not directed to children under 13. Markus Moenig does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## Your choices

- Disable diagnostic sharing in your OS settings if you do not want to send crash data.
- Contact Markus Moenig to request deletion of support correspondence.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will update the date above.
